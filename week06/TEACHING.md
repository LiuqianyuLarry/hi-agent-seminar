# Your Week 6 seminar

In Week 5, you built a workflow that could pause, survive a crash, and reopen a saved CONV-3 temperature report. Today an engineer opens that draft and asks two practical questions: can we really say the bearing failed, and what does the manual require next? Over two 50-minute sessions, you will budget the prompt, separate old checklists from today's facts, build an evidence packer, pull clean SQL rows, compare CAG and RAG while filtering and ranking manuals, and audit the final cited report.

Choose **Review lecture + code** to walk through the lecture figures alongside the Python code, or **Start seminar practice** to jump straight into the CONV-3 files with explanations on demand. At 50% you will have built and tested the evidence packer; by 100% you will have written or repaired four functions and signed off on a grounded engineering report.

Type your code or edits in chat and Mentor Liu will save them in your session. Everything in the core seminar runs locally with Python 3.11+ and SQLite; optional comparisons (benchmarking, local sentence encoders, PostgreSQL/pgvector, and a live DeepSeek wording test) are available in the course menu.
